Heart Disease Prediction

A model that estimates heart disease risk from 13 clinical measurements. It was trained and tested on the heart dataset, with duplicate patients removed so the results reflect patients the model has not seen.

84.9%
accuracy on unseen patients (cross-validated)
0.91
ROC AUC of the final ensemble
302
unique patients after removing duplicates
